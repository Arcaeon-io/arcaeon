## What this number cannot say

It is a static read of source. A server that logs through a platform the scanner cannot see (a logging config file, a sidecar, an HTTP gateway) scores lower than it deserves. The scanner says so in every grade's `blind_spots` and the report repeats it.

It grades one check for TS/JS and says nothing about SSRF, exec, or secrets in those servers. `no-handler-found` is our blindness, not their fault. Reachability is capped at depth 2 from the handler; deeper records are reported as absent.
