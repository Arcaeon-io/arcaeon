$title

A pin is a ledger's head recorded with a witness: how many rows the ledger had and its chain at that row, with the publisher's time and the witness's time. Each pin file under the served root is checked first, the same strict rules and pin chain arcaeon verify --witness reads, and the answer is VERIFIED, BROKEN or COULD NOT LOOK for that file.

$main

A pin says what the witness recorded, not that a ledger is fine today. To compare a ledger with a pin, open its receipt and run the command there against your copy of the ledger.
